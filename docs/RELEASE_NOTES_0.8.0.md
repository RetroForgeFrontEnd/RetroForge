RetroForge 0.8.0 beta

PS3 and PS4 games, online play, your own music and radio, and a lot more for your retro library.

WHAT'S NEW

PS3, PS4 and Switch
- PS3 games open in the RetroForge PS3 Player (a separate download, built on RPCS3). Add DLC from a game's page.
- PS3 online: Settings → Online and friends signs you in to RPCN (a free, fan-run PS3 network), makes an account or signs you out. Games log in by themselves.
- PS4 games: point RetroForge at your own dumped game folders; titles come from the game's own data, and they open in the RetroForge PS4 Player. DLC folders can be added from the game's page. The guide explains how.
- Switch Play Online: host a room from the game's page, and other players on the same Wi-Fi see it and tap to join. Up to 4 players.
- Each player app has a RetroForge neon icon with the console's name.
- You need your own games, keys and firmware from your own consoles. Nothing of Sony's or Nintendo's is included.

Music, radio and films
- A Music tab: songs on your phone, in a folder you choose, or on your PC over Wi-Fi (Find on my network, no typing addresses). Albums with their cover art, Play all, Shuffle.
- Now Playing: moving visuals, big round Play, Previous and Next, Shuffle, Repeat and an equaliser. Music keeps playing in the background, even while you play a game in the built-in player.
- Radio: popular stations where you are, search for any station worldwide, and keep favourites. No account needed.
- The film player has new controls in RetroForge's colours, with subtitles and an equaliser.
- Network drives for games, films and music are kept apart.

Play together
- Netplay for up to 4 players on the same Wi-Fi. On Android 17, RetroForge asks for the new local network permission.
- Virtual link cable: trade and battle in Game Boy and Game Boy Color games between two devices on the same Wi-Fi.
- Save swap: keep the built-in player's saves in step between your devices.
- Tournaments for game nights: knockouts, leagues, groups and double knockouts.

Your library
- RetroAchievements in the built-in player, with the list and badges in the pause menu and progress on the game page.
- Save states with pictures: make, load, rename and delete them in the pause menu or on the game page.
- Backlog and playtime: mark games as playing, finished or up next, with charts per game, system and week.
- Smart collections that keep themselves up to date (by genre, decade, developer, players and your play), and a Gaming passport with your play history and milestones.
- Game notes, guides and maps on the game page and in the pause menu.
- Files: a file browser on the Retro tab.
- Kiosk mode and child lock: a PIN for Settings, deleting and the ROM Manager.
- Input profiles: button layouts for a system, one controller or one game. A controller test in Settings.
- HD texture packs from a game's page (N64, PSP, Dreamcast, GameCube/Wii), and Free up memory in Settings.
- Retro news on the Links tab: emulator releases, retro gaming news and modding.

ROM Manager
- Identify games against No-Intro and Redump lists, and rename them (with undo).
- Fix ROM headers, with the originals kept.
- Compress GameCube games to RVZ, 3DS games to Z3DS, and Dreamcast GDI to CHD.
- ROM hacks: "(patched)", "(romhack)" and "(mod)" files count as hacks, and you can mark any game as a hack on its page. Soft patches beside a game are applied when it plays.
- Save importer: bring PS1, N64 and cartridge saves from other emulators into the built-in player.

Look and feel
- A condensed pause menu with Back working the same in every part.
- Settings headings stand out, and Playing is split into Saves, Online and Controls.
- Tabs run from orange on Home to cyan on the last tab, with a soft glow.
- Frame skip and a sound buffer setting in the pause menu, and Pass the controller to play as player 2.
- 3DS games can open in CitraVR on Meta Quest, for full 3D.

Updating
- Installs over 0.7.1 without uninstalling; games, saves and settings are kept.
