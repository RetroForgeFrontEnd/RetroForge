WHAT'S NEW

PS4
- The RetroForge PS4 Player is out (an early preview, built on shadPS4): https://github.com/RetroForgeFrontEnd/RetroForge-PS4-Player/releases. Put your own dumped PS4 game folders in a ps4 folder, tap Play, and the game opens in the player. Back opens its pause menu, and Quit brings you back to RetroForge.
- The first time, Android asks you to let the PS4 Player access all files, so it can read your game folders.
- Get it, for the PS3 and PS4 Players, now opens each player's own download page.
- PS4 emulation on a phone is new: most games are slow or don't start yet. Sonic Mania runs at about 40% speed on a Galaxy Tab A9+.

Moves and turbo (built-in player)
- Pause menu → Moves and turbo. Turbo: any button presses itself again and again while you hold it.
- Move lists for more than 35 fighting games, among them Street Fighter II, Alpha and III, Mortal Kombat 1 to 4, The King of Fighters, Fatal Fury and Real Bout, Samurai Shodown, Darkstalkers, Killer Instinct, Tekken 3, Marvel vs. Capcom 2 and Primal Rage, with specials, supers and finishers.
- Each move shows its inputs as arrows. Tap a move to put it on screen (up to 4) and tap it to do it; ⇄ turns it round when your fighter faces the other way.
- With a controller: hold Select (View on an Xbox pad) and press the move's last button, e.g. Select + HP for a Hadouken.
- Each game button can be moved to any pad button, kept per system, and a game on another system uses that version's buttons.
- Cheat codes too, such as Contra's 30 lives.
- Your own lists: put a text file per game in the player's moves folder.
- Moves are off during netplay and link-cable play, so nobody uses them against another player.

Box art and guides
- Scrape all artwork (Retro tab, and Settings → Covers and bezels): box art for every game that has none, from libretro first and then ScreenScraper.
- Find a walkthrough, on a game's page under Guides and maps, searches GameFAQs for the game.

Looks, music and films
- Settings → Looks and sounds → Size: RetroForge's words, logos and pictures from 70 to 150%.
- Your music in the pause menu, under Resume: what's playing, with Previous, Play/Pause and Next.
- Films with DTS and Dolby TrueHD sound now play with sound.
- On a phone, the film player's buttons wrap onto a second line instead of squashing, and the EQ panel scrolls.

Languages
- Ukrainian, a seventh language: Settings → Language, or Android's own per-app language setting.
- Portuguese is now Brazilian Portuguese throughout.

Fixes
- Copying a game from a network drive keeps going when you leave RetroForge, with progress in a notification and a "ready, tap to play" when it's done.
- A network drive no longer drops the connection when the PC says a file isn't there.
- Console game lists have Scan for new games, which looks through every game folder, network drives too.
- An Xbox pad's View button is Select in a game instead of pausing.

You need your own games, BIOS, keys and firmware. Nothing of Sony's or Nintendo's is included.

Problems or ideas: https://github.com/RetroForgeFrontEnd/RetroForge/issues
