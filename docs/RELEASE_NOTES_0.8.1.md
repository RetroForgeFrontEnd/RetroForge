HELP WANTED: TESTERS FOR THE GOOGLE PLAY VERSION

RetroForge is coming to Google Play, and Google needs at least 12 people to test it for 14 days before it can go public. If you have an Android phone or tablet and a Google account, you can help:

1. Join the testers group: GOOGLE_GROUP_LINK
2. Then open the test link on your Android device and tap "Become a tester": PLAY_TEST_LINK
3. Install RetroForge from Play, open it now and then, and keep it installed for the 14 days.

Your email stays private: only the developer sees the group's members, and RetroForge itself collects nothing (privacy policy: https://retroforgefrontend.github.io/RetroForge/privacy/).
